Expected: with θ = 0 and orthogonal tones, coherent detection beats noncoherent by roughly 1 dB at the same BER. Non-orthogonal tone spacing raises the BER of both FSK receivers (noncoherent needs Δf = k/Tb, coherent only Δf = k/(2Tb)). A carrier phase offset hurts coherent receivers (output scales by cos θ, vanishing at 90°) but leaves envelope/energy detectors untouched.
